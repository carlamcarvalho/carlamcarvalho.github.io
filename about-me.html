About me

I've always been curious about people: how they live, what they believe, and why they do what they do. That curiosity took me to study Anthropology at ISCTE, and then to a master's in Human Biology and Environment at FCUL, where I worked in human genetics.

Anthropology taught me that the best way to understand someone is to go and see how they live. Science taught me to look for evidence before drawing conclusions. I still use both every day.

In 2019 I joined NTT DATA as a QA analyst on EDP's knowledge platform. I moved on to business analysis and then became the Product Owner. It turned out product work is a lot like fieldwork: listen to people, find the real problem, and test before you believe. Since then I've worked on products for BMW and Salvador Caetano, and today I'm a Product Manager at Eneida.io. The full version is on my Work page.
